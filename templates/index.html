Quick Reference

Card Format

Each card is represented by a rank followed by a suit:

  • Ranks: A (Ace), K (King), Q (Queen), J (Jack), T (Ten), 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2
  • Suits: h (Hearts), s (Spades), d (Diamonds), c (Clubs)
  • Examples: Ah = Ace of hearts, Td = Ten of diamonds, 2c = Two of clubs

Position Abbreviations

  • utg: Under the Gun (first to act pre-flop)
  • utg+1: Under the Gun+1
  • mp: Middle Position
  • mp+1: Middle Position+1
  • hj: Hijack
  • co: Cutoff
  • btn: Button
  • sb: Small Blind
  • bb: Big Blind
